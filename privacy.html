Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Atlanticca respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we may collect, how we use it and the choices and rights available to you.

1. Who we are

Atlanticca is a UK-based business providing market development, representation and consultancy services connecting Galician tourism businesses and destinations with the British market.

For privacy enquiries, you can contact:

Andrea López
Atlanticca
Email: andrea@atlanticca.uk

2. Information we collect

We may collect personal information that you choose to provide when you contact Atlanticca by email or otherwise communicate with us.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your organisation or company name
  • Your job title or role
  • The content of your enquiry or correspondence
  • Any other information you choose to provide to us

3. How we use your information

We use personal information only where we have a legitimate reason to do so, including to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for information
  • Discuss potential business relationships or collaborations
  • Provide and manage our services
  • Communicate with existing or prospective clients and partners
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website and business

4. Our lawful basis

Where applicable, we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis for processing personal information relating to business enquiries, communications and potential or existing professional relationships.

Where another lawful basis is more appropriate, we will rely on that basis and explain it where required.

5. How we receive information

We normally receive personal information directly from you, for example when you contact us by email.

In some circumstances, we may receive professional contact information from publicly available business sources or professional introductions where this is relevant to our legitimate business activities.

6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We may share personal information where necessary to operate our business, provide our services, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

Where third-party service providers process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that they handle that information appropriately.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to maintain appropriate business records and deal with any potential disputes or legal obligations.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

8. International transfers

Some of the service providers we use may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that any transfer of personal information is carried out in accordance with applicable data protection law.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Request the transfer of your information in certain circumstances

To exercise a relevant right, please contact us at andrea@atlanticca.uk .

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

Atlanticca's website is a simple static website. We do not currently use the website to set non-essential advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, tracking technologies or other non-essential cookies in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, appropriate consent mechanisms will be provided.

For more information, please see our Cookies page.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our business, website or applicable data protection requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about how Atlanticca uses your personal information, please contact us first at andrea@atlanticca.uk so that we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
