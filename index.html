Why Now

Galicia's moment in the British market

The United Kingdom is Spain's largest international source market, with 17.8 million UK trips to Spain in 2024. British visitors to Galicia spent an average of €194 per day in 2025 - nearly double the year before - with year-on-year growth of 14.6%.

Galicia has also had a remarkable moment in British media. Gordon Ramsay filmed a full episode of Gordon, Gino & Fred´s Road Trip: Viva España! and returned with his National Geographic series Uncharted, while Eva Longoria chose Galicia for her own programme Searching for Spain. Awareness of the region in Britain is growing.

Yet some of Galicia's most distinctive destinations remain largely unknown to British travellers. Ribeira Sacra, with its dramatic landscapes, heroic vineyards, monasteries and gastronomy, is a particularly compelling example.

That gap between what Galicia offers and what the British market knows about it is where Atlanticca operates.
