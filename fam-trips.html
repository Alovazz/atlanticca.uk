Familiarisation Trips

The best way to understand a destination is to experience it.

We curate small, focused familiarisation trips for carefully selected UK travel trade, introducing them to the people, places, products and experiences that make Galicia distinctive.

What we do

  • Identify relevant UK travel trade participants
  • Design tailored itineraries
  • Coordinate local suppliers and experiences
  • Arrange meetings with tourism businesses and producers
  • Provide ongoing support throughout the trip
  • Follow up commercial opportunities afterwards

Small groups. Relevant contacts. Genuine experiences. A more meaningful way to introduce Galicia to the British trade.
